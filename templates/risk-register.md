# Risk Register — Appendix A.4

Every risk carries a **trigger** (observable condition with a date) and a
**pre-committed fallback** (the decision, already made). **Mitigation** is
what you do *now* to make the risk less likely; the fallback is what executes
if the trigger fires anyway. Score likelihood and impact each low/medium/high
and spend mitigation effort where the pair is worst. One **owner** per risk —
in a team, one name, not "everyone". Review weekly: fire triggered fallbacks,
retire dead risks, update the review date. A register whose dates stop moving
has become ceremony.
